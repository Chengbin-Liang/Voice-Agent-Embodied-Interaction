## 📏 From model results to system results

The [benchmark guide](05-Voice-Agent-Benchmarks.en.md) separates basic answers, turn decisions, multi-turn and multi-party interaction, behavioral instructions, and tool/task completion. A successful tool call, the user hearing correct information, and completion of the environment task are different events and should be evaluated separately.

Survey entry points: [WavChat](SURVEYS.en.md#s01) · [SpeechLM Survey](SURVEYS.en.md#s02) · [Full-duplex survey](SURVEYS.en.md#s03) · [DuplexSurvey](SURVEYS.en.md#s04). Hierarchy numbers in different surveys serve their respective taxonomies; retain the original definitions.

Voice tool calls produce task requests. Connecting a robot also requires executable skills or action policies, state feedback, and cancellation handling.
