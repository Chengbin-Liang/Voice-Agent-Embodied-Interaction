## 🏷️ Label guide

⭐ Read first marks resources that help establish a concept or reproduction path, with a specific reason. Other entries are not assigned a reading rank.

`Academic` identifies universities and academic research institutions; `Industry` identifies company teams; `Academic + Industry` identifies joint affiliations; `Independent Lab` is listed separately. Labels follow author affiliations or the project's maintainers and link to supporting sources. Unconfirmed affiliations are left unlabeled.

Publication badges link to proceedings, journals, or author pages. `arXiv` identifies the linked preprint record; `Project / Technical Demo` identifies a project or demonstration. “Author-reported” means acceptance is stated by the authors in the preprint record. Cite the final original version where available.

Stars and citations are snapshots from 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-03, with dates retained per entry. `OA` is OpenAlex and `S2` is Semantic Scholar. Coverage and record versions can differ: counts are not added together, and missing values are not zero. Stars for shared repositories apply to the whole repository. These counts aid discovery, not quality scoring or reading order.
